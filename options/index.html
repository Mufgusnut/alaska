Why late August

Big antlers, salmon runs and real sunsets

By the end of August, bull moose have grown their full racks. Salmon are crowding the streams near Ketchikan and Juneau, so bears and eagles come down to feed. The midnight-sun days are over, so there's a proper sunset over the mountains, which is perfect for a birthday toast on the balcony.

Crowds and fares also start to ease once school is back in session.

Before we book

Making the most of a balcony

Book two side by side

Ask for adjoining balconies. Many ships have dividers between balconies that the cabin steward can open, so it becomes one long private deck for all four.

Pick the right side

Heading north one-way (Crown and Coral Princess), a starboard (right-side) balcony faces the coastline. Heading south on Viking, pick port (left) side. On round trips, both sides get their turn.

Tell them it's a 70th

Mention Diane's birthday when booking. Most lines will bring a cake to dinner, and you can pre-order balcony breakfast, flowers or champagne for the big day.

Glacier Bay or a fjord?

Six of our options have permits for Glacier Bay, where park rangers come aboard to narrate. The others visit Tracy Arm, Dawes Glacier or Hubbard Glacier, which are just as dramatic.

Book excursions early

Floatplanes, helicopters and the White Pass train sell out in August. Most lines open excursion booking months ahead, and you can usually cancel for a refund up to a few days before.

Passports for everyone

Every option stops in or starts from Canada. Make sure all four passports are valid through spring 2028.
