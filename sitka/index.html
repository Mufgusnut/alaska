Sitka: bears, eagles & onion domes

Russian America's old capital sits beneath the snow-capped peaks around Sitka Sound. It's quieter and more charming than the big ports, and full of wildlife and history. We have eight hours, from 8 am to 4 pm.

Good to know

Before we book

Docking. Eurodam normally docks at the Sitka Sound Cruise Terminal about 5–6 miles from town, with a free shuttle. Check the Visit Sitka cruise calendar closer to the date in case it's a tender day.

Allow time. Leave 30 minutes for the shuttle back before all aboard.

Prices. Holland America refunds in full if it cancels a tour, and its Best Price Guarantee gives 110% of the difference as onboard credit if you find the identical tour cheaper.
