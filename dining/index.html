Worth the extra

Specialty restaurants

Prices are per person (2026) plus a 20% service charge. With the Have It All package, one specialty dinner each is included.

Pinnacle Grill

$52 pp
SteakhouseDeck 2Lunch & dinnerBirthday pick

Holland America's signature chophouse: Wagyu and Double R Ranch steaks, seafood and an excellent wine list, in a quiet, elegant room.

TryTable-side Caesar salad, jumbo lump crab cakes, American Wagyu strip loin or filet mignon, honey-glazed Chilean sea bass, and the "Not-So-Classic" Baked Alaska.

Reserve for embarkation night before sailing for a complimentary bottle of sparkling wine. Lunch is about $25 on sea days.

Tamarind

$39 pp
Pan-Asian & sushiDeck 11Dinner

High up with ocean views, Tamarind serves Southeast Asian, Chinese and Japanese dishes, plus sushi from the Morimoto by Sea sushi menu. Reviewers call it a standout.

TrySatay sampler, pork bao buns, wok-seared lobster, crispy duck, Panang red curry chicken, tuna pizza and the mango posset.

Canaletto

$25 pp
ItalianLido, Deck 9Dinner

Relaxed Italian dinners in a section of the Lido, with house-made pastas and small plates to share. The best value of the specialty restaurants.

Everyday dining

All of these are included at no extra charge (drinks aside).

The Dining Room

Included
Main restaurantDecks 2–3, aftBreakfast · lunch · dinner

A grand two-deck room at the back of the ship, serving multi-course dinners with nightly changing menus. Breakfast and lunch are open seating.

Dinner timesPick fixed seating (about 5:00 or 7:30 pm, the same table and waiters nightly) or open dining (about 5:15–9:00 pm, reservations possible) when you book. Open dining is easiest for a group of four.

One morning there's an Alaska Brunch: blueberry pancakes, salmon chowder, and a Kodiak steak and egg skillet.

Alaska on the menu

Holland America served about 125,000 pounds of fresh Alaska seafood fleetwide in 2025.

Wild Alaska seafood

Salmon, halibut, cod and black cod appear across the menus all week.

Glacier Bay pea soup

Dutch split-pea soup served on the bow while you watch the glaciers. A beloved Holland America tradition.

Alaska Brunch

Blueberry pancakes, salmon and potato chowder, and a Kodiak skillet, once per cruise in The Dining Room.

Alaska cocktails

The Sitka Spritz and Inside Hand, created by bartender Sam Ross, and Glacial Ice cocktails at the Ocean Bar.

Local beer

Alaskan Amber, Icy Bay IPA and Killer Whale Pale Ale.

Live cooking shows

Two per cruise, hosted by the executive chef and the cruise director.

Drink packages (per person, per day)

  • Have It All · ~$65/day: Signature drinks package (drinks up to $12, 15 a day), one specialty dinner, Surf Wi-Fi and $100 excursion credit. About $1,820 for all four of us for the week.
  • Signature Beverage Package · $55.95: cocktails, wine, beer and specialty coffee up to $12 a drink.
  • Elite Beverage Package · $60.95: the same, but drinks up to $16.
  • Quench (non-alcoholic) · $17.95: sodas, juices, specialty coffees and mocktails.
  • Every adult in a cabin must buy the same drinks package, and a 20% service charge applies to drinks.

Tips for dining well

  • Book early. Reserve the Pinnacle Grill for Aug 28 and Tamarind for Aug 31 in Holland America's online planner as soon as it opens.
  • Birthday. Note Diane's 70th on the booking, tell the maître d' on day one, and order a cake through Holland America's gift shop.
  • Dress code. Smart casual every night; two "Dressy" nights call for a cocktail dress or jacket. Holland America's dress guide ↗
  • Dietary needs. Allergies and special diets can be noted in advance. Tell the waiter on the first night too.
  • Port nights. Juneau (until 10 pm) and Icy Strait Point (until 10 pm) are good nights to eat late or ashore.
