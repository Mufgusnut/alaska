Skagway: ride the White Pass

The gold-rush town at the top of the Inside Passage, and the one port this route has that the Eurodam's doesn't. We have thirteen hours here, from 7 am to 8 pm, for the White Pass railway, the Yukon and a boardwalk main street. Here's every Holland America excursion in Skagway, with prices, how active each one is, and how well it suits Mom.

On our own, for free (or close to it)

Skagway is small and flat. Seven blocks of gold-rush Broadway are a national historical park, and most of its museums cost nothing.

White Pass & Yukon Route train on a trestle · Nils Öberg, CC BY-SA 3.0
Klondike Gold Rush park visitor centerFree

In the old railway depot at 2nd & Broadway. The visitor center, its museum, the Mascot Saloon museum and the Moore House need no tickets. Expect roughly 9:00 am–4:30 pm; check the hours on the day.

Jeff. Smiths Parlor MuseumFree, timed ticket

Soapy Smith's saloon, on 2nd Avenue just west of Broadway. Tickets are handed out at the visitor center on the day, so stop there first.

The Broadway boardwalkFree

Seven flat blocks of false-front buildings and wooden sidewalks. The Arctic Brotherhood Hall, with its driftwood front, is at 2nd & Broadway.

Red Onion SaloonFree to enter · brothel tour ~$10

201 Broadway. Restaurant and bar from 10 am, no reservations. The 20-minute brothel-museum tours run 10 am–5:30 pm and are up about 25 steps.

The Days of '98 Show~$25–30

Alaska's longest-running stage show: songs, can-can and Soapy Smith. About an hour, at Eagles Hall, 6th & Broadway.

Skagway Museum~$2–5

In the stone McCabe College building at 7th & Spring.

Gold Rush Cemetery & Lower Reid FallsFree · shuttle or taxi

The graves of Soapy Smith and Frank Reid, with the falls a short path beyond. It's about 1.5–2 miles from the docks, so ride there. The ground in the cemetery is uneven.

Yakutania PointFree

An easy shoreline trail across the footbridge by the airstrip, about a mile from Broadway, with picnic benches and views down the inlet.

Skagway Brewing Co.Pay as you go

4th & State. The Spruce Tip Blonde Ale is the local signature. Klondike Brewing Company has a taproom downtown too.

The city bus$5 all day · $3 each way

Runs between the docks and downtown from 7 am to 9 pm on cruise days. Pay the driver.

Good to know

Before we book

Passports. Every tour that goes on to Fraser, Bennett Lake, Carcross or the Yukon crosses into Canada, and so do the Klondike Summit minibus and the photography and bike tours. Carry passports on the day. We'll all have them anyway, because the cruise starts in Vancouver. Holland America lists no passport requirement for the plain Summit Scenic Railroad, where nobody leaves the train.

The summit is colder. The top of the White Pass is usually 10–15°F colder than town, and often windy or in cloud. Bring a warm layer for the train even on a mild day.

Book the train early. The White Pass trains are what people come to Skagway for, and with three ships in port the good departure times go first. Book in My Account on Holland America's site as soon as excursions open. Booking direct with the railway looks a little cheaper (roughly $150–165 for the summit trip; check when booking), but Holland America's $189 boards at the dock and the ship waits for its own tours.

Which dock. Skagway has four big-ship berths: Railroad Dock forward and aft, the Broadway Dock and the Ore Dock. Ours isn't assigned yet. The Broadway Dock is about a five-minute level walk from town; the Ore and Railroad docks are more like 15–20 minutes, about three-quarters of a mile.

The Railroad Dock rockslides. The mountainside above the Railroad Dock has been shedding rock since 2022, and on the latest reports we found (2025) the dock was still closed to people on foot. Ships at the forward berth have sent passengers ashore by small boat or bus instead; the aft berth, the Broadway Dock and the Ore Dock aren't affected. We haven't been able to confirm how this stands for 2027. If we are given that berth, expect a short tender or shuttle instead of a walk. Tours still meet on the pier. Check the Navigator app nearer the date.

Getting around. The city bus is $5 for the day or $3 each way. For places farther out there's an on-demand shuttle, (907) 612-0902, at $5–15 depending on distance.

Mushers' Camp runs Tuesday to Thursday. Mushers' Camp & S'mores Roasting only operates on those days. August 31, 2027 is a Tuesday, so it runs for us.

No ranger-led town walks. Older guidebooks promise free hourly walks with a park ranger. The National Park Service says it no longer offers them. The Street Car tour or our own stroll with the park's free museums does the job.

Weather and weight. Helicopter tours are often cancelled for low cloud, and guests are weighed, with a surcharge at 250 lb and over. Holland America refunds in full if it cancels a tour.

Prices. These are Holland America's "from" prices per adult as shown in November 2026. Its site doesn't say which season they are for, so 2027 prices may differ.
