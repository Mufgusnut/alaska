Dining on the Koningsdam

Every restaurant, café and bar on board: what to order, what it costs, when to go, and a dinner plan for all seven nights, starting with Mom's birthday dinner at the Pinnacle Grill as we sail out of Vancouver.

Seven nights, seven dinners

Our dinner plan

A plan that works around the port times. The Koningsdam and her two sister ships have the most restaurants in Holland America's fleet, so this uses four of them and leaves three nights for the included dining. Prices are for all four of us, including the 20% service charge. Four paid dinners is generous; Canaletto is the easy one to drop. Book the specialty restaurants in Holland America's online planner before sailing.

Worth the extra

Specialty restaurants

Prices are per person (2026) plus a 20% service charge. With the Have It All package, one specialty dinner each is included. Recent reviews say the specialty food on this ship is better than expected and costs well below what other cruise lines charge.

Pinnacle Grill

$52 pp
Steakhouse & seafoodDeck 2Lunch & dinnerBirthday pick

Holland America's signature steakhouse, with an Alaska regional menu on Alaska sailings. The March 2026 dry dock added movable tables, which makes a table for four easier to arrange.

TryTable-side Caesar salad, clothesline candied bacon, jumbo lump crab cakes, American Wagyu strip loin or filet mignon, honey-glazed Chilean sea bass, and the "Not-So-Classic" Baked Alaska or the Jacques Torres chocolate soufflé.

Reserve for embarkation night before sailing for a complimentary bottle of sparkling wine. Lunch is about $25 on sea days.

Sel de Mer

$55 pp
French seafood brasserieDeck 2DinnerAbout 54 seats

A small, permanent seafood restaurant found only on the Koningsdam and her two sister ships. Older reviews call it "Rudi's Sel de Mer"; it is the same place. A 2026 Alaska review singled out the chocolate hazelnut volcano.

TryBaked escargots, steak tartare, whole Dover sole meunière, seafood cioppino, fruits de mer spaghetti, broiled lobster tail, and the salted caramel St. Honoré or the Jacques Torres chocolate hazelnut volcano.

It is the smallest restaurant on board, so book it early. It is also the dearest cover charge, which makes it the best use of a Have It All dinner.

Tamarind

$39 pp
Pan-AsianDeck 10, aftDinner

High up at the back of the ship, Tamarind serves Southeast Asian, Chinese and Japanese dishes every night. The Tamarind Bar is next door for a drink before or after.

TrySatay sampler, shrimp tempura, pork bao, wok-seared lobster, crispy duck, Panang red curry chicken, wasabi-soy beef tenderloin and the mango posset.

Canaletto

$25 pp
ItalianLido, Deck 9Dinner

Relaxed Italian dinners in a section of the Lido Market, with pastas and small plates to share. The best value of the specialty restaurants. One 2026 reviewer praised the osso buco; another liked the lobster and shrimp ravioli.

TryVeal and sage meatballs, burrata, grilled tiger shrimp, casarecce pesto Genovese, Chianti-braised short rib, tiramisù. Broiled lobster tails are +$19.

Morimoto by Sea Sushi Bar

À la carte
SushiDeck 10, beside TamarindOnly a few seats

A tiny sushi counter with Iron Chef Masaharu Morimoto's menu. It used to be called Nami Sushi, and some deck plans still say so. You pay per dish, plus 20%; Holland America does not publish the prices. A 2026 review praised the spicy lobster pad Thai.

TryToro tartare, tuna pizza, sticky ribs, angry lobster pad Thai, a poke bowl, nigiri and signature rolls, and the white chocolate lime ganache.

Good for a light sea-day supper or a few plates before a show, without committing to a full dinner.

Morimoto by Sea pop-up

~$55 pp
JapanesePop-up, at least 1 nightIn Tamarind or the Pinnacle Grill

A full Morimoto dinner menu that takes over Tamarind or the Pinnacle Grill at least once per cruise. On Alaska sailings it features fresh Alaska fish; past menus have included black cod yuzu and halibut XO.

The night is only announced on board. Check the Navigator app on day one and book right away.

Included in the fare

Everyday dining

All of these are included at no extra charge (drinks aside), except where a card says otherwise.

The Dining Room

Included
Main restaurantDecks 2–3, aftBreakfast · lunch · dinner

A two-level room at the back of the ship, joined by a staircase, serving multi-course dinners with menus that change every night. Breakfast is served daily and lunch on sea days. Recent reviewers rate it good rather than the highlight of the ship.

Dinner timesPick fixed seating (about 5:45 or 8:00 pm, the same table and waiters nightly) or open dining (about 5:15–9:00 pm) when you book. Exact times are set for each sailing. Open dining is easiest for a group of four with tours to work around.

One morning there's an Alaska Brunch: blueberry pancakes, salmon chowder, and a Kodiak steak and egg skillet.

About The Dining Room ↗

Lido Market

Included
BuffetDeck 9~6 am – late

The big casual buffet, with stations for bread, salads, comfort food, world dishes, a carvery and desserts. Expect breakfast about 6–11 am, lunch 11:30–2, dinner 5:30–8 and late snacks after 9 pm. It's where lunch happens on boarding day.

Crew serve you at each station, so lines form at peak times, and some reviewers find the choice a bit repetitive. Go a little early or late. An Alaska Salmon Bake is held here once a cruise.

About Lido Market ↗

Grand Dutch Café

Food included
Dutch caféDeck 3, AtriumNot on the Eurodam

The food is free and the drinks cost extra. Dutch pea soup, open-faced and herring sandwiches, Dutch pancakes and stroopwafels are complimentary; specialty coffees and 16 Dutch and Belgian beers (four on tap) are for purchase.

A good light lunch or mid-afternoon stop. It gets crowded on boarding day and at busy times.

Royal Dutch Tea

Included
Sea daysAbout 3:00 pmThe Dining Room

Holland America's afternoon tea, with premium teas, scones, savory bites and Dutch-inspired sweets, served in the Dining Room on sea days. Ours are Sun Aug 29 and Fri Sep 3. The Lido Market also sets out a simpler tea about 3–4 pm.

It is popular, so go a little early.

Club Orange

Paid upgrade
Private dining roomDeck 2Breakfast & dinner

An optional programme you buy for the whole cruise. On this ship it comes with its own small restaurant, serving breakfast and dinner from an expanded Dining Room menu with no reservations. It is free for Neptune and Pinnacle Suite guests; for everyone else Holland America quotes the price when you ask.

We'd skip it. With four specialty dinners planned and open dining the rest of the week, we would hardly use it.

Only in Alaska

Alaska on the menu

Holland America's fresh fish programme brings local fish from port to plate in under 48 hours, and its wild Alaska seafood is independently certified.

Wild Alaska seafood

Salmon, halibut, cod and black cod appear across the menus all week, with dishes by Masaharu Morimoto among them.

Glacier Bay pea soup

Dutch split-pea soup served on deck while you watch the glaciers on Wed Sep 1. A beloved Holland America tradition.

Alaska Brunch and Salmon Bake

Each happens once per cruise: the brunch in The Dining Room, the salmon bake in the Lido Market.

Alaska cocktails

A list of Alaska cocktails created by bartender Sam Ross, including Glacial Ice cocktails on glacier day.

Local beer

Alaskan Brewing's Amber Ale, White Ale and Juneau Juice IPA, and Denali Brewing's Bachelor Brew lager, Twister Creek IPA and Single Engine Red (the 2026 list).

Live cooking shows

Alaska dishes cooked live on the World Stage, then served in The Dining Room that night.

Drinks

Bars & lounges

Happy hour is usually 4–5 pm at select bars, typically the Ocean Bar and Billboard Onboard, with drinks at half price. Deck numbers are from the deck plans; a couple of small venues may have changed in the March 2026 dry dock, and we say so where that applies.

Crow's Nest

Deck 12

Windows right around the bow at the very top of the ship, the best indoor spot for glaciers. It has a bar, the café, games and puzzles, and it is busy most of the day, so claim seats early on Glacier Bay morning.

Ocean Bar

Deck 2, Atrium

A cocktail lounge by the atrium with live music and a small dance floor. Meet here for a toast before the birthday dinner; the Pinnacle Grill is on the same deck.

Grand Dutch Café

Deck 3, Atrium

The place for a Dutch or Belgian beer, four of them on tap, with free Dutch snacks alongside.

Tamarind Bar

Deck 10, aft

Quiet, with ocean views, Asian-inspired cocktails and sake. Ideal before or after Tamarind, or for a nightcap.

Sea View Bar

Deck 9, aft

By the adults-only Sea View pool at the back of the ship. The best spot for the birthday sail-away toast as we leave Vancouver and pass under the Lions Gate Bridge. There is a smoking area nearby.

Billboard Onboard

Deck 2

Two pianists playing chart hits, sing-alongs and all-request sets. Ask them for "Happy Birthday" for Diane. More seating was added in 2026.

Rolling Stone Lounge

Deck 2

A live band playing rock, pop and country, with a small dance floor. It is next door to Billboard Onboard, and new seating between the two lets you watch either.

B.B. King's Blues Club

Decks 2–3

A Memphis blues and soul band in a two-level room with a big wooden dance floor, something the Eurodam does not have. The dance floor for the birthday night. Seats go quickly at busy times.

Lido Bar & Panorama Bar

Decks 9 & 10

The Lido Bar is poolside under the glass roof; the Panorama Bar is one level up, overlooking the pool. Handy with a burger or pizza.

Sun Bar & Casino Bar

Decks 11 & 3

The Sun Bar is an open-deck bar where smoking is allowed. The Casino Bar is inside the casino.

Notes & BLEND

Check on board

Older guides list Notes, a whiskey bar between Billboard Onboard and the Rolling Stone Lounge, and BLEND, a ten-seat room on Deck 2 for a blend-your-own-wine class. That part of the ship was rebuilt in the March 2026 dry dock and Holland America no longer lists either, so check on board before counting on them.

Drink packages (per person, per day)

  • Have It All · $65/day: Signature drinks package (drinks up to $12, 15 a day), one specialty dinner, Surf Wi-Fi and up to $100 excursion credit. About $1,820 for all four of us for the week.
  • Signature Beverage Package · $55.95: cocktails, wine, beer and specialty coffee up to $12 a drink.
  • Elite Beverage Package · $60.95: the same, but drinks up to $16.
  • Quench (non-alcoholic) · from $17.95: sodas, juices, specialty coffees and mocktails.
  • Every adult in a cabin must buy the same drinks package, and a 20% service charge applies to drinks. Room service and the minibar are not covered.
  • Tastings cost extra. A beer tasting or mixology class was about $15 and a wine tasting about $25–39 in 2023; expect a little more now.

Tips for dining well

  • Book early. Reserve the Pinnacle Grill for Aug 28 first, then Sel de Mer for Sep 1 (it is small), Tamarind for Sep 2 and Canaletto for Aug 31, in Holland America's online planner as soon as it opens.
  • The pop-up. The Morimoto by Sea night is only announced on board. Look in the Navigator app on day one and move a booking if it suits us better.
  • Birthday. Note Diane's 70th on the booking, tell the maître d' on day one, and order a cake through Holland America's gift shop.
  • Have It All. If we buy it, each of us gets one specialty dinner at the Pinnacle Grill, Sel de Mer, Tamarind or Canaletto. Spend it on Sel de Mer or the Pinnacle Grill, not Canaletto.
  • Dress code. Smart casual every night. Two "Dressy" nights call for a dress, or a collared shirt and slacks; jackets are optional. The nights are confirmed in the Navigator app once on board. Holland America's dress guide ↗
  • Dietary needs. Allergies and special diets can be noted in advance. Tell the waiter on the first night too.
  • Port nights. Juneau (until 10 pm) is the one night to eat late or ashore. Skagway sails at 8 pm and Ketchikan at 7 pm, so those dinners are on board.
  • Fitting it together. The day-by-day timings are in our trip planner, and the rest of the ship is in the Koningsdam ship guide.
