Chapter 1

Is this the cruise for us?

An honest scorecard, drawn from Holland America's own materials and recent passenger and press reviews (2023–2026). The sailing is the 7-Day Alaska Inside Passage, round trip from Vancouver, voyage K759.

It's a great fit if we want…

  • Glacier Bay and Skagway on one trip. A full day in the national park, plus the White Pass railway, the one famous Alaska outing the Eurodam route can't offer.
  • A newer, fresher ship. Built in 2016 and refreshed in March 2026 with new carpets, seating and gym equipment.
  • The most choice at dinner. Five specialty restaurants, including the French seafood brasserie Sel de Mer, plus the free Grand Dutch Café.
  • The best live music in the fleet. Three clubs side by side: Rolling Stone Lounge, Billboard Onboard and B.B. King's Blues Club.
  • Easy port days. No port call starts before 7 am, Ketchikan runs 11 am to 7 pm, and in Vancouver we walk from the hotel straight onto the ship.
  • A lower fare. A verandah is about $1,909 per person, $405 less than the Eurodam.

Think twice if…

  • We want Sitka, Icy Strait Point or Victoria. This route visits none of them, and there is no evening port call after Glacier Bay.
  • We're counting on Tracy Arm. The ship only pauses for half an hour at the mouth of the fjord. Seeing the glaciers there means a $364 small-boat excursion.
  • We want a quiet, mid-size ship. About 2,650 guests, roughly 550 more than the Eurodam on a hull only about 40 ft longer. The pool deck, buffet and blues club get busy.
  • Cabin size matters. A standard verandah is about 228 sq ft including the balcony, against about 254 on the Eurodam.
  • We want easy flights. Boston to Vancouver has no well-timed nonstop, so it's a one-stop day each way and about $150 more per person than flying to Seattle.
  • We love a classic promenade. There is a wraparound deck, but lifeboats block much of the view and there are few deck chairs.

Bottom line: If we want Glacier Bay, the White Pass train, a newer ship with more restaurants and music, and a birthday that starts by walking downstairs from the hotel onto the ship, Koningsdam is an excellent choice for Diane's 70th, and it costs less. Plan the birthday dinner in the Pinnacle Grill on sail-away night, and book midship verandahs on Deck 5, 6 or 7.

Costs, packages and the budget

For all four of us in two verandah cabins. The cruise fare is Holland America's sampled price. Everything else is a planning estimate. The trip planner lets us change the choices and see the total move.

Excursions use our picks in Chapter 6: Mendenhall Glacier & Whale Quest ($274), the White Pass Summit train ($189) and the Skagway Street Car tour ($59), and the Misty Fjords cruise ($239). Good to know

  • The border: US customs inspects everyone inside Canada Place before boarding, so the Alaska ports feel like domestic stops. Coming back we clear Canadian customs in the terminal, then US preclearance at the Vancouver airport before the flight; allow three hours there.
  • Flying into Canada: a passport is all we need. The optional Advance Declaration (in the ArriveCAN app, up to 72 hours ahead) speeds up the kiosk at the airport.
  • Dress code: smart casual. There are two "Dressy" nights, usually the first sea day (Sun Aug 29) and one later evening, often the last sea day (Fri Sep 3). Think cocktail dress or a collared shirt and jacket; no tux needed.
  • Dinner times: choose fixed seating (about 5:45 or 8:00 pm) or open dining (about 5:15–9:00 pm) when booking. Open dining is easier for a group of four.
  • Skagway: the docks are a flat walk of about 5 to 20 minutes from Broadway, depending on the berth. The city's SMART bus is $5 for the day. If we are given the forward Railroad Dock, expect a short shuttle or tender instead of walking, because of rockfall above that pier.
  • Ketchikan: six ships call that day. If Koningsdam is at a downtown berth, Creek Street is a flat 5–15 minute walk; the berth is confirmed nearer the date, and a tender or the Ward Cove dock north of town is possible. All aboard is about 6:30 pm.
  • Juneau: the afternoon call (1:30–10:00 pm) is shared with four other ships, so book tours ahead. All aboard is about 9:30 pm.
  • Passports on tour days: any Skagway tour that crosses into Canada (Fraser, Carcross, the Klondike Highway summit) needs a passport in the daypack.
  • Clocks: Vancouver is three hours behind Boston. The ship goes back one more hour to Alaska time on the way north and forward again on the way south.
  • Money in Vancouver: Canadian dollars, but tap-to-pay cards work almost everywhere, including the SkyTrain gates. Tip 15–20% at restaurants. Check that phone plans include Canada, and use airplane mode at sea.
  • Accessibility: accessible cabins, wheelchair help at Canada Place (ask Holland America before sailing) and elevators throughout the terminal, though the walk through it is long. Several excursions have wheelchair-accessible versions.
  • Bags on the last night: put them outside the door on the evening of Fri Sep 3. We collect them in the terminal on Saturday morning.

Pack for late August

  • Weather: Vancouver ~72–75°F and mostly dry; Juneau, Skagway and Ketchikan ~55–62°F; the White Pass summit 10–15°F colder; Glacier Bay on deck ~45–50°F; rain likely somewhere.
  • A waterproof hooded jacket, fleece, a warm hat and light gloves, and waterproof walking shoes.
  • Two dressy outfits, a swimsuit for the covered Lido pool and the hydro pool, and binoculars for everyone.
  • Passports, a small daypack, motion-sickness remedies for the open-water stretch on Day 2, and a birthday banner.
  • No plug adapter needed: Canada uses the same outlets as home.

Chapter 8

Fine print & things to double-check

We researched this in November 2026, nine months before sailing. These are the details most likely to change.

  • Tracy Arm or Endicott Arm: the itinerary still says Tracy Arm for Aug 30, but after the August 2025 landslide Holland America ran every 2026 departure of the small-boat excursion up Endicott Arm to Dawes Glacier instead. Expect the same in 2027 unless Holland America says otherwise. Either way the ship itself only pauses at the fjord mouth; Holland America doesn't spell this out, but the half-hour schedule leaves no time for anything more.
  • Sail-away time: 3:00 pm (check the final time). The port agent's calendar shows 4:00 pm for Aug 28. The boarding window and the latest time to be in the terminal move with it, so go by the check-in time in the Navigator app.
  • Excursion line-up & prices: these are the "from" prices on hollandamerica.com in November 2026. Holland America doesn't label the season, and 2027 prices may differ.
  • Berths and tendering: none of the Alaska berths are assigned yet. Ketchikan has five ships overlapping at the downtown docks between 11 am and 1 pm, so one may anchor and tender or use Ward Cove.
  • Skagway's Railroad Dock: rockslides above the dock have kept its forward berth closed to people on foot since 2022, with guests shuttled or tendered past the slide zone. We found no word on whether that changes for 2027. Tours still meet on the pier.
  • Juneau's Goldbelt (Mount Roberts) Tram: Holland America listed it as closed through Sep 29, 2026. Check whether it reopens for 2027 before booking either tram tour. The Mendenhall visitor center may also keep shorter evening hours.
  • Dressy nights: not published. Usually the first sea day (Aug 29) and one later evening, often the last sea day (Sep 3), but it could be the Glacier Bay evening instead. The daily programme on board has the final word.
  • Glacier Bay route: which glaciers we see (Margerie, Grand Pacific, Johns Hopkins, Lamplugh, Reid) is decided on the day, depending on ice and wildlife. Johns Hopkins Inlet is normally closed to cruise ships during seal pupping until about the end of August, so Sep 1 may be the first day the captain can go in.
  • Hotel rates and the exchange rate: the Vancouver rates are our estimates, not quotes, and the US-dollar figures assume C$1 ≈ US$0.72. Confirm the Pan Pacific's bag-to-ship service and its fee (reported as about C$5 a bag), and ask the Fairmont Waterfront how much of its renovation is finished.
  • Flights: 2027 schedules aren't final. The JetBlue nonstop is seasonal and may not fly every day of the week, and one-stop times are based on 2026.
  • Taxes, port fees and the fare: the ~$290 per person for taxes and fees is our estimate; Holland America shows the real figure at checkout. The $1,909 fare was a sampled price and will move.
  • Prices on board: crew appreciation is $18 per person per day for verandahs (since June 2026), plus 20% on drinks and specialty dining. Have It All for summer 2027 is $65 per person per day. Décor package prices date from 2024.
  • Small venues: the BLEND wine-blending room and the Notes whiskey bar appear on older deck plans, but we couldn't confirm either after the March 2026 dry dock, so we haven't counted on them.
