Victoria: gardens & harbour lights

The last stop is an evening in Victoria, British Columbia, from 8 pm to midnight. The Parliament Buildings glow with thousands of lights, and Butchart Gardens is lit up after dark. It's also the farewell "Dressy" night, so plan an early dinner.

Our top three for Diane

If we only read one page

With an 8 pm arrival, everything happens after dark, which is when Victoria is prettiest. Eat an early farewell dinner, then head out.

Good to know

Before we book

Butchart hours. Summer hours have run until about 10 pm, with viewing until 11. There are no fireworks on Friday, Sep 3 (they're on Saturdays). Confirm Holland America's evening tour runs on our date.

Packing night. Luggage goes outside the cabin door by about 10 pm. Pack before going ashore.

Passports. Bring them ashore. Canada requires them.

Prices. Holland America refunds in full if it cancels a tour, and its Best Price Guarantee gives 110% of the difference as onboard credit if you find the identical tour cheaper.
