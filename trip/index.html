Getting there & back

Flights, transfers and the hotel

Flying out

Boston Logan → Seattle-Tacoma, nonstop on Alaska Airlines, Delta or JetBlue, about 6 hours 20 minutes westbound.

  • Aim for a morning departure around 8 am, landing around 11:30 am Pacific with the afternoon free.
  • Fly the day before (Friday the 27th). Never fly in on the morning of a cruise.
  • Rough fare: ~$350–550 per person round trip in economy. Late-summer fares climb, so book in spring.

Holland America sails from Smith Cove Cruise Terminal, Pier 91 (2001 W. Garfield St.), northwest of downtown. With four people and luggage, an XL rideshare or a pre-booked car is easier than the train. Confirm the pier on your cruise documents.

Flying home

  • Eurodam arrives at 7:00 am on Sat Sep 4, and you'll be off by about 8:00–9:30 am at your assigned time.
  • Book a flight leaving after about 12:30 pm. SEA → BOS is about 5 hours 15 minutes, landing around 9:30 pm Eastern.
  • Port Valet (free): sign up on board and your checked bags go straight from the ship to your airline. You get boarding passes before you leave the ship. It's for flights after 11 am with participating airlines.

Birthday splurge

The Edgewater

Built out over Elliott Bay at Pier 67, where the Beatles once fished from their window. Waterfront rooms look toward the Olympic Mountains, a lovely kickoff for a 70th.

~$450–700 / night · 15 min to Pier 91

Classic & convenient

Mayflower Park Hotel

A charming 1927 hotel in the heart of downtown, steps from the Westlake light rail and a short walk to Pike Place. It runs a cruise shuttle to both piers (about $15 per person in 2026).

~$250–350 / night · cruise shuttle

Closest to the pier

Lower Queen Anne / Seattle Center

Hotels like the Mediterranean Inn, near the Space Needle, are about 2 miles from Pier 91. It's a quieter evening and the shortest ride on boarding day.

~$200–300 / night · 10 min to Pier 91
